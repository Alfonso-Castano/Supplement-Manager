<objective>
Activate the high-end-visual-design taste skill, then apply targeted animation polish: tune the .fade-up cubic-bezier easing, verify hero entrance and nav scroll transition, and add hover states to Testimonial cards, Pricing tier tiles, and the VideoSection play button. No new animation libraries or animation types — CSS only.

Purpose: Animation easing is the most overlooked quality signal on a marketing page. The current `ease` keyword produces a generic browser default. Replacing it with `cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)` produces the snappy spring deceleration used by Linear, Vercel, and Apple — the reference tier. Hover states make the page feel responsive and tactile.

Output: A landing page with polished, Apple-tier scroll animations and complete hover states on all interactive and semi-interactive elements.
</objective>
