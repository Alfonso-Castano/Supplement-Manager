Title: Renal Transporter-Mediated Drug-Biomarker Interactions of the Endogenous Substrates Creatinine and N(1)-Methylnicotinamide: A PBPK Modeling Approach
Journal: Drug Metabolism and Disposition. 2022 Jul;50(7):956-968
DOI: 10.1124/dmd.122.000856

Key findings relevant to NMN (nicotinamide mononucleotide / N1-methylnicotinamide):
- N1-methylnicotinamide (NMN) is an endogenous substrate of renal organic cation transporters OCT2 and MATE1/2-K
- Drugs inhibiting these transporters (trimethoprim, pyrimethamine, cimetidine) reduce NMN renal clearance, elevating plasma NMN levels
- Metformin is also an OCT2/MATE substrate and may interact with NMN at shared renal transport sites
- Both NMN and metformin affect cellular energy metabolism pathways (NAD+/AMPK); combined use may have complementary effects on glucose regulation
- Pharmacokinetic modeling indicates transporter-mediated drug-NMN interactions are clinically measurable
