Title: The Effect of Mushroom Extracts on Human Platelet and Blood Coagulation: In vitro Screening of Eight Edible Species
Authors: Poniedzia?ek B, Wiktorowicz K, Szecówka M, Dworczyński A, Pawlak-Bus K, Leszczynska-Rodziewicz A, Regula J, Kopciuch D, Nowakowska E
Journal: Nutrients. 2019 Dec 20;11(12):3040
DOI: 10.3390/nu11123040

Key findings relevant to lion's mane (Hericium erinaceus):
- Eight edible mushroom species were screened for antiplatelet and anticoagulant activity in vitro
- Hericium erinaceus (lion's mane) showed minimal direct antiplatelet activity compared to Auricularia auricularia-judae and Pleurotus eryngii
- "None of the mushroom extracts altered the monitored coagulation parameters (prothrombin time, prothrombin ratio, and International Normalized Ratio)"
- Clinical interaction data for lion's mane with anticoagulants is lacking; cautious use recommended in patients on anticoagulant therapy
